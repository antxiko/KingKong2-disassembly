Findings

What turned up when the cartridge was taken apart. Each one says where it comes from and how it is checked: seen in openMSX, checked (drawn from the ROM and compared with a dump) or from the code (read in the listing, not played).

Three endings, and the best one is not the obvious one

The game counts the days (0xC127, one every 2048 frames: about 34 seconds) and the times you have continued after GAME OVER (0xC16E, p01:66DD). On finding LADY KONG, p06:A837 chooses the ending:

conditionending
366 days or moretoo late: «AMY could do nothing: KING KONG has just died»
otherwise, 51 continues or moreAMY operates and KING KONG comes back to life
fewer than 51the same, and a baby KONG as well

The texts are the ROM's (p06:AD44-AF40). From the code.

The level trick

With TAB and an F key held down when the game starts (on pressing SPACE during the demo), p01:67F3 leaves in 0xC11E the level you start with: F1 10, F2 20, F3 30, F4 40 and F5 50; p00:5388 copies it to LEVEL.

Seen in openMSX (tools/omsx_truco.tcl): without the trick, LEVEL 00; TAB+F1, 10; TAB+F4, 40, in RAM and on the panel.

Every enemy has its weapon

p09:BB14 decides whether the SHOT1 weapon does anything to the enemy, and p09:BAE8 looks separately at the sword of light, the axe and the harpoon:

  • the axe only hurts GRIZZLY, and GRIZZLY only falls to it;
  • the harpoon, only B F MAN and D F MAN;
  • WHITEFIRE and WHITEOGRE, only with the sword of light;
  • CLUBMAN, with the ROD; BIGSPIDER, with the knife; REDSPIDER, with the club or the claw; SLIPSLIME, GARGOYLE, MUDMASK, BOGGEDMAN, BLOWMIRE, MIREWORM and MIREFROG, only with the claw;
  • TWINMIRES, with anything, but only if you carry the おまもり charm (p09:BBAB);
  • THE NASTY, BADPRIEST, BIGFLOWER, MUDARMOR, FIRETOTEM, GRASSOGRE and record 0x3D take nothing from close combat;
  • THICKSKIN, MR. RUINS, CAVERNMAN and BUCKLER, depending on the side they are hit from.

Reading p09:BAFC it looked as if the sword of light hurt nobody: the scf at p09:BAFF always leaves the «yes» flag. From the code.

The axe is not sold: BOWLER drops it

No shop sells the axe (p12:B548). BOWLER drops it every 16 kills if you do not carry it (p03:BBA9), except on screen 0x22, where every 5 it leaves someone who talks. Every enemy has its loot (p03:BA66 and p03:BA06) and its count (0xDFC0-0xDFDB, which p06:BF86 sets at the start):

  • BATT, meat every 6 kills; RATT, やくそう every 3 on screen 9; DUNGROLL, 99 stones every 5; CAVEMAN, 10 fireballs every 10;
  • FIRETOTEM, the club every 12 if you do not carry it; GRASSOGRE, the ROD every 16; THICKSKIN, かいてん every 5; CAVERNBIG, 99 きいろいいし;
  • REDSPIDER, ぱぴるす; BIGSPIDER, the wooden key; RED F MAN, the claw; REVOLVER, あぐのす; THE NASTY, the harpoon; BIGGUARD and SAVAGE, the iron key;
  • after the bosses, event 5, which removes the scenery of that type;
  • on screen 0x6E, when the WATCHMAN count runs out a passage appears.

From the code.

THE NOT moves the opposite way to you

Lined up on a tile, THE NOT reads the player's keys (0xC107) and chooses the opposite direction with the table at p02:9201: up for down, left for right. One time in four it goes at random; once it is hit, always at random (0xDFB5). BOWLER, in its step 1, goes with the keys (p02:8986), and the worms (PIGWORM, BUCKLER, GRASSOGRE) move away from where Mitchel is facing if any key is pressed (p03:B190). From the code.

Five bosses that are not sprites

BIGFLOWER, TWINMIRES, THE NASTY, BADPRIEST and REVOLVER are not sprites. On entering their screen, p00:4BD0 uploads their figure raw to page 1 (p14: [mirror][rows][bytes][VRAM] and the bytes), and a second time mirrored if it carries the flag. Every time they move or open their mouth, their routines copy it to page 0 with LMMM and TIMP. Before that, p00:446E saves 64 × 64 dots of the background at (0x90, 0x60) of page 1 to put it back later.

Checked: tools/jefes.py rebuilds what p00:4BD0 uploads on the seven screens (0x29, 0x31, 0x34, 0x53, 0x57, 0x5C and 0x5D) and gives 0 bytes different against the dumps.

What the island hides

  • 15 passages: p06:BD9D changes screen when Mitchel steps on a tile 0xC0-0xC3 (list at p06:BE16). Several are covered by rocks that only the てむさ spell removes.
  • 37 pieces of scenery on 30 screens can be removed, each one by its event (p09:B3FD; p15:BD3A dissolves them dot by dot with the VDP's PSET command).
  • Removing the one on screen 0x0B makes the sword of light appear (p15:BD80).
  • On screen 0x0D, with the wooden key, you go through to 0x70 (p01:7F87).
  • On screen 0x88, as long as you do not carry ささやき, a figure is painted (p06:BBEF, p06:BC05).

All of it marked on the map, from the tables.

The durian and the one who follows Mitchel

Using the durian on the right screen and spot (p01:7D33) sets state 6 of the game: the durian on the ground, the weapons gone, 30 frames, a tune, and to screen 0x5F. From there, an eight-sprite figure (0xC600) follows Mitchel along his path, 22 steps behind (p02:829A). If you change screen it stays waiting until you come back. While it is with you, the radio gives another conversation (0x38 instead of 0x37): «Good work, MITCHEL. We are on our way». From the code.

ROCKMAN and STONEMAN never head for Mitchel at random

Away from his row and column, ROCKMAN and STONEMAN pick a direction every three steps. The code (p02:9440) takes a random number, keeps only bit 2 (and 4, which gives 0 or 4) and jumps to "aim at Mitchel" (p02:945D) if it is 1: it can never be 1, so the branch is never taken from there and the direction is always random among the ones that are open. With and 3 it would come up one time in four. It comes from the code: the comparison is impossible.

The thing that can be pushed

There is a thing that can be pushed (0xC800) and that travels from screen to screen: it starts on screen 8, at (0x50, 0x50), and pushing it over the edge moves it to the next screen (p12:BE68). Only outdoors; on screens 0-2 and on 4 it goes back to its place. From the code.

Three enemies that do nothing

MUDMASK, BOGGEDMAN and BLOWPIPER have names, but their every-frame routine (p01:690D) and their creation routine (p00:5BA6) are a ret. From the code.
