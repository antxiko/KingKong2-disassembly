In the emulator

What is drawn on this website is checked against openMSX with a Japanese MSX2 machine (C-BIOS_MSX2_JP, and -romtype Konami after the -cart). The probes are Tcl scripts that put the cartridge where it is needed by touching the RAM and dump the VRAM, the RAM, the palette and the VDP registers. They are run one at a time.

Tips for seeing it

  • The screen goes in 0xC125; with 0xC201 = 1 the game loads it.
  • Mitchel: 0xC303 is his y, 0xC305 his x.
  • LEVEL is 0xC12E (BCD), LIFE 0xC129, GOLD 0xC135.
  • With TAB and F1-F5 held down at the start, you start with LEVEL 10-50.
  • An MSX1 machine hangs at p00:46DD waiting for the VDP: it needs an MSX2.
